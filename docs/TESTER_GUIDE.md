# PricePulse ZA: tester guide

Thank you for testing. This takes about 10 minutes.

**Test address:** _(will be added after deployment)_

PricePulse compares prices of the same product at different shops. It does not sell
anything. Prices come from the shops' websites and **may differ in store. Always
check at the till.**

## On your Android phone

1. Open the test address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
3. **Sign in** with Google, or create an account with your email and a password.
   If you use TrolleyUp, use the same sign-in.
4. **Confirm your age** by typing your South African ID number and ticking the box.
   The number is checked and not kept.
5. At the top, choose your **Town**, or tap the **target button** to use your
   location. Your location stays on your phone.

## Please try these

1. **Look up a price.** Type `milk` in the search box and tap **Look up price** on a
   product. You should see at least 3 shops, with the cheapest marked **Best price**.
   The red bar at the top counts your items: "Session 1 of 3 today · 1 of 45 items".
2. **Compare sizes.** Tap **Per 100g / 100ml** to compare big and small packs fairly.
3. **Details.** Tap a product name for every shop's price and a price-history chart.
4. **Shopping list.** Look up 4 or 5 products, then open the **List** tab. Change
   quantities with **−** and **+**. **Where to shop** shows the cheapest single shop
   and how much you save by splitting your shop.
5. **Send to TrolleyUp.** On the List tab tap **Send to TrolleyUp**, then follow the
   steps shown to import the file into a TrolleyUp list.
6. **Towns.** Open **Towns** to see what your list costs in nearby towns.
7. **Clear and limits.** Tap **Clear session** on the List tab and start again. After
   3 sessions in one day the app should say you have none left and offer the
   R99 plan. **Please do not pay during testing** unless Joao asks you to.
8. **Offline.** Turn on flight mode and open the app again. It should show your saved
   list and say **Showing saved prices from …**.
9. **Text size.** Make your phone's text bigger (Settings → Display → Font size).
   Everything should still fit and be readable.
