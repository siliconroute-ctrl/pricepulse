# PricePulse ZA: tester guide

Thank you for testing. This takes about 10 minutes.

**Test address:** _(will be added after deployment)_

PricePulse compares prices of the same product at different shops. It does not sell
anything. Prices come from the shops' websites and **may differ in store. Always
check at the till.**

## On your Android phone

1. Open the test address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). The app now
   opens from your home screen like any other app.
3. At the top, choose your **Town**, or tap the **target button** next to it to use
   your location. Your location stays on your phone.

## Please try these

1. **Search.** Type `milk` in the search box. You should see milk from at least
   3 shops. The cheapest shop is marked **Best price**.
2. **Compare sizes.** Tap **Per 100g / 100ml**. Prices now show per 100 g or 100 ml,
   so big and small packs can be compared fairly.
3. **Details.** Tap a product name. You see every shop's price, a price-history
   chart, and a link to the shop's website.
4. **Shopping list.** Tap **+ List** on 4 or 5 products. Open the **List** tab. Change
   quantities with **−** and **+**. Under **Where to shop** you see the cheapest
   single shop, and how much you save by splitting your shop.
5. **Share.** Tap **Share list** and send it to yourself on WhatsApp.
6. **Towns.** Open **Towns** to see what your list costs in nearby towns.
7. **Offline.** Turn on flight mode and open the app again. It should still work
   and say **Showing saved prices from …**.
8. **Text size.** Make your phone's text bigger (Settings → Display → Font size).
   Everything should still fit and be readable.
