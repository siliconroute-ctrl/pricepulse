What PricePulse is

PricePulse ZA compares prices of the same product at different South African shops. It is not a shop. It takes no orders and sells nothing except its own monthly plan.

Prices are collected from public retailer websites and may differ in store. Always check at the till. We do our best, but we cannot promise that any price is correct, current or available in your branch.

Your account

You need to sign in. One person may have one account. Your sign-in is the same one you use for TrolleyUp. Keep your password to yourself.

Age

We ask for your South African ID number once to check your age. Liquor prices are shown only to people aged 18 and over. PricePulse does not sell alcohol. If you are under 18 you need a parent or guardian's permission to use PricePulse.

Free plan

You may start up to 3 lookup sessions a day. In each session you may look up the prices of up to 45 items. An item you have looked up stays counted for that session even if you take it off your list. Clearing a session ends it. The daily count starts again at midnight, South African time.

Monthly plan

  • R99 a month for unlimited lookup sessions, each with up to 45 items.
  • Paid by card through PayFast. The plan renews every month until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time in the app (Account, then Cancel my plan). No more payments are taken, and you keep the plan until the end of the month you have paid for.
  • Payments already made are not refunded for part of a month, except where the law gives you that right. Nothing here limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.
  • If we change the price, we will tell you at least 30 days before your next payment, and you may cancel.
