Privacy Policy

Version 1. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Short version: we never keep your ID number, we never see your card number, your location stays on your phone, and you can delete your PricePulse data at any time.

Who we are

PricePulse ZA is run by Silicon Route (Joao de Gois), South Africa, the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Contact: admin@trolleyup.com.

What we collect and why

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with Google or with an email and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase and is the same account you use for TrolleyUp. Firebase holds your email address. The PricePulse server stores only your account number, not your name or email.
  • Your age, from your ID number. We ask for your South African ID number to check whether you are 18 or older, because the app shows liquor prices. The number is sent to our server over a secure connection, checked, and then thrown away. It is never saved and never written to our logs. We keep only:
    • whether you are 18 or older;
    • if you are under 18, the date you turn 18, so liquor prices can be shown from that day;
    • a scrambled fingerprint of the number, made with a secret key. It lets us see that an ID number has already been used, so one person cannot open several accounts. It cannot be turned back into your ID number.
  • Your lookups. The products you look up, your list and quantities, and how many sessions you have used today, so the limits and your list work.
  • Your plan. If you pay for the monthly plan: that you are subscribed, until when, a reference from PayFast, and the amount and date of each payment. Payment is made on PayFast's pages. We never see or store your card number.

What stays on your phone only

  • Your location, if you tap the location button. It is used on the phone to pick the nearest town and is never sent to us.
  • Your chosen town, colour setting, and a saved copy of your last list so the app works without a connection.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not show adverts. We do not read your TrolleyUp lists. Our server logs do not record internet (IP) addresses.

Who else handles your information

  • Google Firebase (sign-in). Google may store data outside South Africa.
  • PayFast (payments), if you subscribe.
  • Our hosting company, which runs the PricePulse server.

Sending your list to TrolleyUp

"Send to TrolleyUp" makes a file on your phone. Nothing is sent to TrolleyUp until you import that file there yourself.

Under 18

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you enter your ID number. Liquor prices are hidden for people under 18.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. In the app, go to Account and tap Delete my PricePulse data: your age check, fingerprint, sessions and list are removed straight away. Payment records are kept without your account number for as long as tax law requires. Your sign-in itself is shared with TrolleyUp; to remove it too, email us.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email admin@trolleyup.com. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will ask you to agree again.
